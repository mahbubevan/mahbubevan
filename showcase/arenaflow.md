# ArenaFlow — Multi-Vendor Sports Venue Marketplace

> **Laravel · Multi-role booking · Pricing rules · Wallets · Vendor commissions**

[← Back to GitHub profile](../README.md)

## The product
ArenaFlow is a configurable sports venue booking marketplace serving platform administrators, venue owners and customers.

## Engineering highlights
- **Three-sided platform:** administrative control, venue-owner management and customer discovery/booking experiences.
- **Scheduling rules:** duration constraints, buffers, maintenance blocks, availability and dynamic pricing rules.
- **Reservation integrity:** documented server-side checks, transaction-based reservation handling and temporary slot holds designed to prevent double-booking.
- **Marketplace finance:** configurable commissions, vendor wallets, withdrawals and payment driver architecture.
- **Broader operations:** KYC, rewards, reviews, localization/RTL and customer check-in workflows documented in the project.

## Technology & architecture
Laravel · MySQL · Role/Permission Controls · Booking Engine · Payment Drivers · Wallet Ledger · Background Jobs

## What this demonstrates
Marketplace domain modeling, booking conflict prevention, flexible pricing, business-rule separation and multi-role product architecture.
