# Khondokar Surgical — Sales & Inventory Operations

> **Laravel 13 · Livewire 4 · Inventory · Reporting · Business workflows**

[← Back to GitHub profile](../README.md)

## The product
A custom system for surgical-product business operations, focused on product stock, sales and management reporting.

## Engineering highlights
- **Inventory traceability:** stock movements and stock allocation data structures.
- **Stock operations:** dedicated stock service and UI workflows for opening stock, adjustments, damaged stock and expiry-sensitive inventory.
- **Sales operations:** sales model, invoice-related views and a dedicated sales reporting service.
- **Operational visibility:** stock reports, sales reports, customer views and global search.
- **Admin access:** the dependency stack includes Spatie's Laravel Permission package for role/permission support.

## Technology
PHP · Laravel 13 · Livewire 4 · MySQL · Spatie Laravel Permission · Reporting Services

## What this demonstrates
Real-world inventory concerns, stock integrity, product lifecycle awareness and operational reporting—not merely a product CRUD dashboard.

**Code availability:** Private client/business repository. Internal data and implementation details are not published.
