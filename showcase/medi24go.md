# Medi24Go — Healthcare Commerce & Order Management

> **Laravel 13 · Livewire 4 · Sanctum · Reverb · Transactional workflows**

[← Back to GitHub profile](../README.md) · [Visit Medi24Go](https://www.medi24go.com/)

## The product
Medi24Go is a medicine and surgical-item commerce platform combining a customer-facing catalog with ordering and operational workflows.

## Engineering highlights
- **Order lifecycle:** order and order-item models, draft orders, order status history and change logs.
- **Customer updates:** dedicated realtime order update events, with Laravel Reverb and broadcasting dependencies in the application stack.
- **Payments and accounting records:** payment transaction and cash transaction models supporting operational traceability.
- **API-ready architecture:** Laravel Sanctum and an associated private mobile-app repository.
- **AI integration work:** service classes for AI-powered medical-upload processing; this is an application feature, not a claim of medical diagnosis or automated clinical decision-making.

## Technology
PHP · Laravel 13 · Livewire 4 · MySQL · Laravel Sanctum · Laravel Reverb · Broadcast Events

## What this demonstrates
Domain-specific order management, event-driven customer communications, API integration and traceable business state.

**Code availability:** Private. No patient/customer records, credentials or proprietary business logic are shared here.
